# Looks (brand.look)

Pick the look from the tone, not by habit (the audit flags a look used in a recent film). For more visual languages, browse the 100-theme gallery of saas-motion-kit (https://tugrawork-creator.github.io/saas-motion-kit/) and port the idea into `engine/src/lib/Media.tsx` → `World` (+ chip/title styling in `Kinetic.tsx`).

| look | Feel | Background | Type | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `soft` | warm, friendly | light gradient, drifting depth-of-field decor (pins/dots/balls) | brand display, ink on light | travel, lifestyle, consumer apps |
| `flood` | bold, confident | brand colour field, slow rings | white display, dark highlights | launches, drops, sports |
| `editorial` | trustworthy, considered | paper + grid, rules | serif titles, outlined chips | fintech, news, B2B |
| `keynote` | premium, "Pro" | dark, single glow, vignette | white display | tech, premium |
